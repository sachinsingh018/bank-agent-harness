# AML investigator prompt changelog

- v1: first investigator prompt (typologies, how to work, evidence rules, decision rules, 12 tool call budget).
- v2: adds a step to compute every sum figure with the new sum_transfers tool and states the answer limits plainly (1 to 50 transfer ids and at most 20 amounts per claim, at most 10 claims and 10 figures, narrative at most 1,200 characters). Why: in run 20261116T014628Z-c9d334 one case stated wrong sums that the arithmetic verifier caught and two first submissions broke the schema caps.
- v3: adds a section saying most alerts are false positives: name and check the ordinary explanation, escalate only on evidence beyond the alert shape, and use the whole confidence range. Also: choose the typology from the observed structure rather than the detector name, keep cited amounts to the transfers a statement mentions (aim for 5 or fewer, totals through sum_transfers), and put statements without transfers in the narrative. Why: in run 20261116T020950Z-beb465 (v2, 3 alerts from the smoke set, which is drawn from the dev split only, decision 0005) the agent escalated all 3 alerts with confidence 0.75 to 0.9 while only 1 was real laundering, got the typology wrong, and every first answer broke the 20 amounts or empty claim limit. Signals that measure how much activity an account has were left out on purpose, because accounts look quiet in the late window for an artifact reason (decision 0006); the prompt says to judge accounts by what their transfers show. Before accepting v3, compare its early and late splits against v2.

# AML reviewer (checker) prompt changelog

- reviewer_v1: first checker prompt for maker-checker. A senior analyst checklist (evidence supports the recommendation, ordinary explanation ruled out, typology matches the structure, confidence fits the evidence, no overstated claims), a 6 tool call budget, and approve only if the findings would hold up. review_message_v1 shows the alert id and the maker's findings as JSON. Not yet measured with a real model (docs/plans/maker-checker.md).

# AML lessons block changelog

- lessons_block_v1: the block added to the first user message when a run uses lessons (runner flag --lessons on, off by default). It lists up to 5 saved reviewer lessons from other alerts and says they are general guidance, not evidence about this alert. Not yet measured with a real model (docs/plans/corrections-memory.md).
- v4: restructured for open reasoning models such as Nemotron: short always-apply rules up front, a numbered procedure with a call plan (about 8 calls, submit by the 11th), a points based evidence score mapped to recommendation and confidence bands, a typology guide that separates GATHER-SCATTER from one-sided FAN-IN or FAN-OUT, simpler figure rules (copy sums from sum_transfers, count equals the number of ids, at most 2 figures, zero is fine), one worked submission example with made up ids, and a final self-check. Why: in dev run 20261118T234719Z-cd5c6c (98 dev alerts, decision 0005) judgement was near chance and calibration poor (ECE 0.33); most failures were figures (count mismatches 23 first answers, wrong sums), empty claims (13), figures over 50 ids (11), step limits from looking up accounts one by one (get_account 179 calls), and GATHER-SCATTER over-used. Signals about how busy an account is stay out (decision 0006). Not yet run; owner chose to ship the prompt without a test run.
- v5: v4 plus three more structural evidence signals (multi-hop movement in a short time, detector parameters well beyond thresholds, several receivers paying on to the same next account) and a softer false positive reminder that says not to close by default and that missing laundering is a serious error. Why: in run 20261119T031633Z-66f106 (first 5 dev alerts) v4 closed all 5 and missed the one real positive; the model rarely reached the escalate score. Signals about how busy an account is stay out (decision 0006).
